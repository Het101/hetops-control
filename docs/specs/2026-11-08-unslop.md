## Direction

- **Product.** A command center for one owner's three Kubernetes servers: what needs them now, what changed, and the few guarded actions to take.
- **Character.** Watchful, unhurried, exact.
- **World.** A fire lookout over a forest at night: graphite dark, ember for fire, living green for growth.
- **Moments.** The forest on Home, the estate at a glance and the one rich visual; and one main chart per page, drawn with care. Everything else is lists and figures.
- **Signature.** The ember. It appears once per need, exactly where you act on it, and nowhere else.

## Decisions

- **Ramp:** graphite dark (rail #0f0f10, page #141415, card #19191b, raised #232325, hairline = primary text at 8%); text #ededed / #a1a1a6 / #85858b (muted only for meta).
- **Accent:** ember is the one chrome accent and means "needs you". Living green (#4cc38a family, keeping our tested #46d6b0) is content only: the forest, the live dot, a recovered state. It never colours chrome or every bar.
- **Type:** IBM Plex Sans for the interface, IBM Plex Mono for figures, times and meta. Fraunces and Plus Jakarta Sans go. Weights 400 and 500, 600 at most. Sentence case everywhere, no tracked uppercase, tabular figures.
- **Depth:** surface steps and hairlines; no dual shadows, no glows. A floating dialog keeps one soft neutral shadow.
- **Radius:** tags 4 to 6, rows and buttons 8 to 10, cards 12 to 16, panels 16; nested radius = outer minus padding.
- **Copy:** no trailing periods on headings, labels or buttons; legends become a hover title or go; one need, said once.
- **Charts:** one accent per chart, and it marks what matters (a failing hour, the current value); the rest are neutral steps.
- **Logo:** the wordmark "HetOps Control" in Plex Sans 500 and a flat ember glyph, no glow, no animation.
- **Motion:** one curve `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`; the forest's wind stays (it shows the page is live); ember particles only on a branch that needs you; no other loops; hovers are colour or surface steps.

## Plan (PRs, in order)

1. **Foundations.** Tokens, fonts, depth, radius, rail and logo, top bar, page headers, labels, status text, focus. Every page picks it up.
2. **Home.** The need said once with its action; the forest as the moment; one main chart; compact lists for deploys, backups and sites (neutral bars, ember only for failing).
3. **The other pages.** Timeline, Alerts, Apps, Topology, Backups, Security, Settings, Server; drawer, dialog, toasts.
4. **Filters in the URL** (Timeline and Apps).
5. **Ctrl+K palette:** servers, apps, pages, and actions through the existing dialog.
6. **Incident threads:** one incident's events (fell, actions, recovered) folded into one row with heat().
7. **Staging actions:** argo.sync and argo.refresh on staging behind the same ValidatingAdmissionPolicy as the lab (hetops-k8s-lab, owner merge).

Each PR: unit and e2e tests updated, screenshots at 1366 and 390 checked against the checklist, CI green, rollout PR for the owner.
