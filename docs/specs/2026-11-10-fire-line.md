# Fire line

The owner chose (10 Nov) direction 1 of the rework board (claude.ai/artifact/P1NRA5mNTQYpXj6XikGcZT, after an
impeccable shape round and an unslop pass): the estate drawn as a forest on a ranger's beat map. It keeps the forest idea
and replaces the look, the page structure and the components.

## Thesis

One map, one register. Every page becomes a zoom level of the map (estate, server, namespace, app) or an entry in the
register (what is burning, what is being put out, what came back). Nothing is a card in a grid.

## The five states

Every mark is in exactly one, and each comes from a signal the center already has (`lib/burn.ts`):

| State | Meaning | Signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | broken, waiting on you | entity status `attention` or `down` (down burns hotter) |
| Rain | being put out | an `ActionRun` for the entity is `running`; or its Argo app is syncing (`sync` OutOfSync with health Progressing); or a burning app's ready pods are climbing toward `podsDesired` |
| Steam | just recovered | a status event to `ok` for the entity in the last 30 min (the agent's RECENT window) |
| Fog | can't see | status `unknown`, or the server is unreachable or never connected |
| Calm | alive | everything else |

A failed run (rain that did not take) shows as fire again, with the run's error in the register.

Colour carries meaning only: fire #FF7A2F (the only orange), rain #7CC0FF, steam #D5DEE6, moss #5FBF8A, fog hatched grey.
Chrome is a near-neutral graphite ramp; the map is the one rich surface.

## World

- Type: Overpass (variable) for the interface, Overpass Mono for figures, times and coordinates; Fontsource, OFL, served by the app.
- Map: contour lines, dashed compartment boundaries (one per server), groves (one per namespace), drawn trees (roots,
  tapered trunk, forked branches, a canopy of individual leaves; size by pods), a river.
- Motion: fire flickers, rain falls, steam rises (all live state); nothing else loops. Reduced motion stills all three.

## Delivery

1. **Foundations and the map** (this PR): tokens and type, `lib/burn.ts`, `lib/grove.ts` (layout and tree geometry), the
   `Map` component replacing the forest on Home (one Home; the Lookout/Watch pick goes) and on the server pages.
2. **The register**: Timeline, Alerts and the queue as one log beside the map; the move button walks fire, rain, steam.
3. **Zoom and fly-to**: a server zoomed in with the selected tree open beside it; Ctrl+K flies the map.
4. **Lenses and phone**: Sites, Security, Backups and Deploys as lenses on the map; the phone layout.
